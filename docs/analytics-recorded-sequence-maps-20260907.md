# Citrus recorded sequence maps

Implemented reusable, outcome-independent sequence extraction in `data-pipeline/projections/recorded_sequence_map.py`. This is an offline mapping/feature layer, not a trained xG improvement or production deployment.

## Coverage and measurements

Export: `scripts/proof/results/recorded-sequence-maps-20260907/`, generated by `scripts/proof/export_recorded_sequence_maps.py`.

- All 116,506 shots in the existing eligible 2025–26 cohort, including 8,348 goals. Existing source exclusions remain unchanged.
- 45,436 shots have at least one eligible prior recorded live event within the window.
- 10,769 shots have at least two prior recorded live events.
- Up to four prior recorded events within ten seconds; these are context limits, not timing/distance scoring buckets.
- Explicit attacking orientation, node coordinates, event IDs, relative times and team relations.
- Pair and anchor-to-shot lateral/longitudinal distance, angle change, crossing and rates.
- Sequence summaries: cumulative recorded lateral displacement, net lateral displacement, reversals, centerline crossings and recorded team changes.

Goals and non-goals receive the same feature extraction. Outcomes are stored separately from `features` and its fingerprint. No assist, scoring-total, current-goal type or highlight information enters the feature payload. Goal versus non-goal outcomes may be used for supervised labels and retrospective stories, not as predictor inputs.

## Interpretation and boundaries

Connections mean "next recorded event," not a measured puck trajectory or confirmed pass. Team ownership is recorded event attribution, not proof of continuous possession. Crossings describe y=0 in the goal-to-goal frame, not necessarily a pass through the slot. No goalie position is observed.

Sequences stop at the existing history selector's boundaries: period changes, goals, stoppages, unknown events/owners and shootouts. Team changes remain visible and are never silently treated as same-team possession. Missing geometry stays missing; it is not bridged by selecting a better-looking older point. Same-clock rates remain unknown. The ten-second/four-event cap means some histories are truncated by design.

Most shots do not have an eligible recent recorded live-event chain under these rules. Their maps contain the shot only. This is explicit incomplete context, not evidence that no pass occurred.

## Verification and examples

Four unit tests passed, covering exact geometry and reversal arithmetic, zero-time rates, outcome/future invariance, stoppage/team/horizon boundaries, missing coordinates, orientation and order rejection. Exported shot/goal totals exactly match the frozen eligible-feature cohort; raw game body hashes match the frozen source manifest. Per-shot feature fingerprints and source body hashes are retained.

The included interactive examples are actual recorded sequences, selected for multiple same-team events and a final cross-centerline movement of at least twenty lateral feet within two positive recorded seconds. They are illustrative, not matched comparisons:

- Game 2025020102, event 808: blocked shot → missed shot → goal. Final recorded movement: 41 lateral feet, 30 longitudinal feet, two seconds, approximately 93.7 degrees angular change.
- Game 2025020008, event 768: shot on goal → missed shot → missed shot → non-goal attempt. Final recorded movement: 20 lateral feet, four longitudinal feet, one second, approximately 81.9 degrees angular change.

The export's separate `examples.json` contains extreme lateral-path illustrations; these must not be presented as representative sequences. Neither set supports causal claims about pass success or goalie recovery.

Next model step: export identical features for earlier seasons, test whether sequence/reversal and geometry interactions improve predictions and calibration, and retain a separate later calibration period. Do not boost xG merely because a plotted sequence looks dangerous.
