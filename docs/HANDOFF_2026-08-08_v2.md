**Fuzzer finding (documented in test comment):** INV-4 as originally authored (universal batch equivalence `fold(A++B) == fold(fold(A), B)`) fails 195/10000 under adversarial shuffling. Root: deliberate gap-halt semantic at `deriveDraftState.ts:184-196` diverges combined-vs-split paths on shuffled input. Combined fold halts on first gap; split fold's second batch B gets a fresh chance. **Not a fold bug.** Real wire pattern is TCP-ordered / monotonic-seq; narrowed invariant covers realistic case. INV-4-EXTENDED added as canary for future fold refactors.
