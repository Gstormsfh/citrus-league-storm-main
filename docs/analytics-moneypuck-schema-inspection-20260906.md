# MoneyPuck file study → independent Citrus work

Reviewed 6 September 2026. The local explanatory dictionary was read in full;
no shot dataset was loaded, no MoneyPuck prediction was fitted, and no model
binary was deserialized. This is study of disclosed structure, not an exact
reconstruction of a private fitted system.

## What was actually scanned

[`data/MoneyPuck_Shot_Data_Dictionary.CSV`](../data/MoneyPuck_Shot_Data_Dictionary.CSV)
contains **124 unique named fields**. The second copy in the saved Citrus checkout
has identical bytes. The checked dictionary SHA-256 is
`77f2e46f77f252d9571f96bbb85230ecb77a795f806769319753b4d92b1c6d6c`.
The [machine-readable inspection](../scripts/proof/results/moneypuck-dictionary-inspection-20260906-1746/inspection.json)
exhaustively partitions the field names and binds the scanner source. Six tests
verify complete/disjoint classification and refusal to inspect arbitrary or
changed dataset/model bytes. It is not an automatic feature allowlist.

The inspected worktree data folders, saved-checkout data/historical folders and
Downloads did not contain the expected MoneyPuck shot CSVs/ZIPs. That is a bounded
filesystem finding, not a claim that no copy exists anywhere. Old inventory
counts describe historical holdings and are not current verified corpus sizes.

There is an important local trap: the preserved utility
[`deep_analyze_moneypuck_model.py`](../scripts/utilities/deep_analyze_moneypuck_model.py)
does not just inspect structure. It fits a regressor to MoneyPuck `xGoal` values.
Its source was inspected, **not executed**. The current instruction excludes
that training/distillation path. Older Citrus artifacts with MoneyPuck in their
filenames are not thereby MoneyPuck's exact model or newly approved baselines.

## What the structure teaches us

| Distinction found in the dictionary | Citrus consequence |
|---|---|
| Current-shot context versus later outcomes | Prior recorded events can inform a predictor; a later rebound, freeze, continuation, on-target outcome or final game result cannot become a pre-shot input. Those may define separately adjudicated first-party labels. |
| Separate predicted shot outcomes | Goal, rebound, freeze and continuation are different targets. The dictionary's sum-to-one note describes an outcome partition; it does not disclose whether a single multiclass learner or several reconciled models produced it. |
| Rebound **on this attempt** versus rebound **generated by this attempt** | Existing prior-shot motion belongs to current chance quality. Creation needs its own future-opportunity target and conditional value; observed flurry accounting alone cannot supply that forecast. |
| Penalty timing, shift context, handedness and recording correction | These reveal potential missing information, not ready-made Citrus features. Reconstruct from our own source receipts, preserve unavailable states, and fit transforms only on earlier training data. |
| Missing-TOI sentinels in an older dictionary | Do not carry sentinel values into Citrus as measured fatigue. A missing shift interval remains unavailable. |

The reread of [MoneyPuck's public methodology](https://moneypuck.com/about.htm)
also distinguishes event-motion features, power-play timing, neutral probability,
talent adjustment and rebound/sequence products. Its
[glossary](https://moneypuck.com/glossary.htm) clarifies the probability populations.
These pages and a dictionary do not disclose every current training decision,
fitted parameter, data correction or source revision. An archival usage note is
not being treated as evidence of present commercial permission.

## What we applied—and what the tests said

The [full public-method gap matrix](analytics-public-method-gap-20260906.md)
maps concepts to actual Citrus code, not feature names in a pitch. Geometry,
shot category, immediate-event movement, rebound angular context, strength,
score and faceoff age already exist in the frozen development lane.

We implemented and measured independent neutral **strength specialists**,
informed by [Evolving Hockey's disclosed design](https://evolving-hockey.com/blog/a-new-expected-goals-model-for-predicting-goals-in-the-nhl/).
The completed matched comparison lost to the retained monotone-group baseline
on both proper probability scores in both earlier development folds. Every
state remains represented, including sparse/unknown pooled fallbacks. This
tested one fixed Citrus implementation—not whether all possible specialist
architectures are inferior, and not whether Citrus beats another site's model.

A separately versioned shooter/goalie-conditioned forecast tests predictive
identity associations while retaining neutral xG for chance-quality and goalie
evaluation. It uses only earlier official-source outcomes and our own fixed
regularization. Its numerical failure and corrected solver are preserved
separately. The [v2 plan](analytics-identity-probability-v2-plan-20260906.json)
explicitly retains retrospective actor-availability and non-cross-fitted
calibration-period stacking limitations. Completion is not automatic promotion.

## Next information to earn, not assume

1. **Penalty-state reconstruction:** PP elapsed time is explicitly unavailable
   in the validated feature projector. Reconcile penalty start/expiry, goals,
   coincidentals, delayed penalties, period transitions and extra attackers
   before calling a count advantage a power play. Faceoff age is not a substitute.
2. **Outcome labels and rebound creation:** establish source-only labels,
   mutually exclusive event definitions, censoring and player-change coverage.
   Fit continuation opportunity and conditional quality separately from direct
   xG; validate each before assigning credit.
3. **Verified shift/handedness/venue joins:** preserve full raw evidence and
   exact clocks/identities; do not invent fatigue, tracked passes or rink knots.
4. **Calibration and end-to-end integration:** keep conditional weaknesses
   visible and retain frozen comparisons. A lower global error or a working
   transport adapter cannot certify forecasts or FPAR.

The broader [method-preservation map](analytics-method-preservation-20260906.md)
remains authoritative for scope: rink effects, Bayesian finishing, opportunity,
joint uncertainty, on-ice/GAR, goalie evaluation, era effects, possession/action
value and the wider research catalog are retained. This is not a flurry-only
rebuild. Official actuals, legacy artifacts and prospective reservations remain
preserved. No production change occurred in this checkpoint.
