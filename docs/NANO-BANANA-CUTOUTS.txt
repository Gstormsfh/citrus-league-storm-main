CITRUS — THE ONE THAT MATTERS
Cut-outs. Transparent background. Nothing else in this file.

WHY THIS ONE

Look at any hero band in the build right now. There is a hard vertical seam
about two thirds across: the arena plate stops and a different room starts.
That second room is the dark-green studio background baked into every mascot
render. The character is not a character sitting on a scene — it is a
photograph of a character in a box, pasted onto a scene.

That seam is on all nine pages. It is the single most "made by a machine"
artifact left in the build, and no amount of new scene art fixes it, because
the scene art is not the problem. The characters are.

Cut them out and the same renders start compositing properly: full body on a
plate, bust in a 46px avatar, reaction pose over a result card, at any size,
against any background, with a real drop shadow underneath instead of a
rectangle of someone else's lighting.

Twelve files. Everything else in the build already works.


================================================================
THE PROMPT
================================================================

Feed it the existing -tor render as the source image. Edit, do not generate:
a regenerated character is a different character and the set falls apart.

--- paste this, then the line for the file you are making ---

Edit this exact image. Remove the background completely and output the
character alone on a fully transparent background (alpha channel, PNG or
lossless WebP).

PRESERVE EXACTLY, DO NOT REDRAW: the face, the eye shape and colour, the
expression, the body proportions, the sweater design and its navy and white
colours, the number on the sweater, the badge, every prop the character is
holding, and the pose. Same low-poly 3D style, same camera angle, same
distance.

PRESERVE THE LIGHTING ON THE CHARACTER: keep the key light from the upper
left and the rim light exactly as they fall now. Do not relight, do not
flatten, do not add a new light.

REMOVE: the dark green vignette, the studio backdrop, the floor, the cast
shadow on the floor, and any glow or haze that belongs to the background
rather than to the character.

The alpha edge must be clean and slightly soft — no white halo, no dark
fringe, no leftover green spill on the outline. Trim tight to the character
with a few pixels of padding. 1024x1024.

--- end ---

================================================================
HOW TO TELL IT WORKED
================================================================

Open the file on a WHITE background, not a dark one. Every failure mode is
invisible on navy and obvious on white:

  green spill        a lime rim tracing the outline
  white halo         a pale outline where the matte was cut too wide
  dark fringe        a grey outline where it was cut too tight
  ghost floor        a faint oval under the skates
  hard alpha         a jagged edge instead of a slightly soft one

If the character's own lighting changed — flatter, or lit from a new side —
reject it. That is a regeneration wearing the right sweater, and it will not
sit beside the other eleven.


================================================================
DROP THEM IN
================================================================

    (into apps/web/public/mascots/, then)
    python3 bake_art.py && python3 build.py

The build reads a `-cut` file in preference to the boxed one wherever a
character sits on a plate, and falls back to what is there now for any that
have not landed. Deliver one or all twelve.


================================================================
WHAT THIS WILL NOT FIX
================================================================

Being straight with you, because you asked for the prompt that closes the
gap and this one does not close all of it.

A club's own app leads with players. Faces, names, numbers, photography of
people who played last night. Yours leads with fruit. That is not a prompt
problem and no image fixes it — it is a positioning choice, and there is a
real argument for the fruit: it is the thing MLSE cannot buy from anyone
else, and every club app on earth already looks like every other club app.
But it is a choice, and Friday is the room where you make it out loud rather
than let a screenshot make it for you.

The other half of "AI slop" was never art either. It was pages that were a
quarter content and three quarters empty navy. Guess the Leaf is fixed — it
shows all seven clue slots now, locked ones priced, so the page reads as a
board instead of a form. The same pass is owed to Luck or Skill, Beat the
Buzzer, and Rank 'Em.
