Monte Carlo Simulation Engine

10,000 Sims • Student-t Copula • Data-Driven Correlations • Antithetic Variates • Brier Calibration
NEW — Quant-Grade
Stage A — Projection Uncertainty Propagation
Per-Player Distributions
5,000 MC samples per player-game. Converts point estimates to full probability distributions with 90% and 50% confidence intervals.
5K Samples
Bayesian Finishing Posterior
Beta-Binomial conjugate prior (α=10, β=90). Updates with actual goals & shots. Stabilizes at 50+ shots. Multiplier capped [0.7, 1.5].
Conjugate Prior
8×8 Stat Correlation Matrix
Empirical inter-stat correlations from 2023–2025 NHL data. Goals↔SOG: 0.55, Goals↔PPP: 0.42, Hits↔PIM: 0.28. Cholesky-decomposed Gaussian copula.
Cholesky
Dynamic Confidence Scores
CV = σ/μ from MC distribution. Confidence = clip(1−CV, 0.05, 0.99). Labels: High (≥0.60), Medium (≥0.35), Low (<0.35).
MC-Derived
Stage B — Matchup Simulation (simulate_matchups.py)
PlayerDistribution
Empirical distributions from per-game stats. Gamma for count stats, Normal for continuous. Confidence-weighted variance inflation: σ′ = σ × (1 + (1 − confidence)).
Per Player
CorrelationEngine
Student-t copula (ν=5) with 15–20% tail dependence. 3 data sources: shift overlap mining (ρ up to 0.65), assist chain boost (+0.20), Kendall’s τ co-movement.
t-Copula
MatchupSimulator
10K simulations per matchup. Antithetic variates (2× efficiency). Stratified sampling by game-count tiers. Vectorized fantasy point calc (10× faster).
10K Sims
Vegas Calibration
Over/Under game totals scale offensive projections. ou_scale = O/U ÷ 6.0, capped [0.80, 1.25]. GSAx adjusts goalie distributions ±15%.
Market Signal
Stage C — Season & Calibration
SeasonSimulator
Simulates all remaining league matchups to compute playoff probability, championship odds, and final standings distributions for every team.
Full Season
BrierTracker
Prediction calibration: BS = mean((p−outcome)²). Tracked per league per week. Ratings: Excellent (<0.10), Good (<0.15), Adequate (<0.20).
Calibration
Output Fields (14 new columns)
mean, std_dev, median, 90% CI, 50% CI, skewness, upside_probability, floor_probability, dynamic_confidence, likely_low/high, confidence_label.
Full Distribution
player_projected_stats → UncertaintyEngine (5K samples) → PlayerDistribution + CorrelationEngine → t-Copula → Antithetic Variates → matchup_simulations (10K sims)
