Machine Learning & Analytics Engine

XGBoost Models • Expected Goals • GAR • GSAx • Bayesian Regression
The Secret Sauce
xG v3 Model (XGBoost)
31 features. Trained on 863K real NHL shots (7 seasons). AUC-ROC 0.817, Brier 0.054. Distance, angle, strength state, rebounds, pass quality & more.
7 Models
xA (Expected Assists)
Tracks passer contribution independently. Pass zone, laterality, immediacy, goalie movement.
Dual Tracking
GAR (Goals Above Replacement)
Composite value metric. 5 components: EVO, EVD, PPO, PPD, Penalties. Bayesian stabilized.
Per Player
GSAx (Goals Saved Above Expected)
Goalie quality metric. Shot-by-shot save probability vs actual. Bayesian regression (C=500).
Per Goalie
raw_shots → Feature Extraction (31 features) → XGBoost v3 Predict → Clip (0.0 – 0.95) → player_talent_metrics

Fantasy Projections Engine

Daily at 6 AM MT — Bayesian Shrinkage • DDR • Finishing Talent • Contextual Adjustments
Daily Pipeline
Bayesian Shrinkage
Weights player history vs league average. GP<10 = 20% trust, GP≥30 = 90% trust. Prevents small-sample volatility.
Stabilization
Defensive Difficulty Rating
Team defense × goalie quality × offense strength. Capped [0.7, 1.3]. Per-opponent adjustment factor.
Opponent Quality
Finishing Talent
Actual goals / xG ratio. Stabilized for shot volume (<50 shots blended). Capped [0.7, 1.5].
Skill Multiplier
Back-to-Back Penalty
5% reduction (0.95x) for second game of back-to-back. Applies to all skater stats.
0.95x
Home Ice Advantage
5% boost (1.05x) for home games. Based on historical NHL home/away splits.
1.05x
Goalie Projections
SV% shrinkage (C=500), fatigue penalty (-0.015), shutout probability, win probability & confidence scoring.
Multi-Factor
Season Stats + Talent Metrics + Schedule → Bayesian Regression → DDR × Finishing × B2B × Home → projection_cache → player_projected_stats
