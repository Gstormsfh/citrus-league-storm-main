Understanding Shot-Level R²

Context

Shot-level R² values of 0.02–0.03 are industry standard for xG models. Individual shots are inherently binary (goal or no goal) while xG outputs continuous probabilities. Even the best public models (MoneyPuck, Evolving Hockey) achieve similar ranges at the shot level.

The real test is aggregation: when you sum xG across a player's entire season, noise cancels out and the signal emerges. That's where our 0.7149 R² and 0.8772 correlation shine.
