The Pipeline

From the NHL faceoff dot to your phone, in 30 seconds.

Six stages, fully owned, end-to-end. Every layer compounds the next.

01
Raw Ingestion
NHL play-by-play, shot coords, shifts, zone entries. 100-IP proxy rotation. Never rate-limited.
863K shots · 7 seasons
02
Feature Engineering
31-feature xG extractor — geometry, context, 10-feature pass module, sequence, rebound.
Pass MOAT · Royal road
03
xG v3 Model
XGBoost gradient boosted. AUC 0.817, Brier 0.054. Bayesian finishing posterior on top.
Daily retrain · Calibrated
04
Correlation Mining
Shift overlap + assist chains + Kendall’s τ co-movement. Three independent signals.
Data-mined, not heuristic
05
Monte Carlo Engine
10K sims, Student-t copula (ν=5), Cholesky-decomposed stat matrix, antithetic variates.
10K× per matchup
06
Your Phone
Hono API, PostgreSQL with RLS, React frontend. 30s live updates, sub-second renders.
PWA · Mobile-first
