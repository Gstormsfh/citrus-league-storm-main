# Reporting references

These are historical editorial references, never live news inputs. The standard synthesizes techniques rather than imitating an author's voice or copying their prose.

- Justin Bourne, Sportsnet, 2023-04-10: [Opportunity Analysis](https://www.sportsnet.ca/nhl/article/opportunity-analysis-how-player-and-puck-tracking-data-help-us-measure-shot-quality/). Distinguish shot volume from shot quality; lateral movement, traffic and location need tracking or reporting evidence.
- Sonny Sachdeva, Sportsnet, 2025-05-13: [Toronto power-play personnel](https://www.sportsnet.ca/nhl/article/should-maple-leafs-change-up-power-play-personnel-for-game-5/). Personnel and puck delivery can explain outcomes, but a suggested change is different from a confirmed practice unit.
- Adam Vingan, Sportsnet, 2026-04-30: [Dostal–Ingram analysis](https://www.sportsnet.ca/nhl/article/analyzing-the-dramatic-goalie-showdown-between-dostal-and-ingram/). Keep short-run performance, season evidence, defensive exposure and scoring support distinct.
- Derek Van Diest, NHL.com, 2026-04-14: [Draisaitl injury update](https://www.nhl.com/news/leon-draisaitl-injury-status-update-april-13-2026). Practice participation does not establish readiness for a game.

# Original examples (illustrative fixtures, not current player claims)

A high-volume shooter's central question is whether shot generation persists if conversion falls. An assist-heavy producer's is exposure to teammates finishing, especially when power-play scoring dominates. A defensive specialist can help hits and blocks while offering little to a points-only roster. A goalie with strong wins and weak save percentage creates a different decision in volume scoring than in ratio categories. Explain those differences before appending career totals.

A dated report that a player practiced changes the next thing to verify: game clearance. It does not create an exact return date. Confirmed first-unit practice deployment changes the opportunity discussion, but does not guarantee regular-season usage or a new projection. An old injury article, a similarly named player, and a proposed lineup in an opinion article must not change the assessment as confirmed news.
